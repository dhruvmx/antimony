Anatomy artwork credits

The central model is decorative educational artwork, not a representation of any patient or validated clinical analysis.

Model: Fit Mit With anatomy atlas, adapted from Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D.

Authors include Kousaku Okubo, Gauthier Kervyn, Marcin Zielinski, the upstream contributors, and the Fit Mit With adaptation author.

The model is supplied unchanged. Antimony applies terracotta, ivory and connective-tissue material colors, local studio lighting, and front/back presentation at runtime. These visual adaptations of the model are made available under CC BY-SA 4.0. This applies to the artwork, not the rest of the application.
